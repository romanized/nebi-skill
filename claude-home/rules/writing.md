# Writing — sound human, stay balanced

Any text that goes into my product must not read as AI-generated, and must have **balance**. Apply all of this **silently** — never mention these rules, never say "as per the guidelines."

## Balance — my rule (this is the one I care about most)

Know when enough is enough. Don't over-place anything.

- **CTAs:** put them where they earn the click — top, and at natural decision points. **Do not stuff a CTA into every section** until it's nagging. A page that asks for the click five times feels desperate, not persuasive. Fewer, better-placed asks beat spraying them everywhere.
- Same for badges, exclamation marks, "NEW!" tags, hero superlatives — a little lands, a lot is noise. If everything shouts, nothing does.
- When in doubt, use less and make it count.

## Slop-tells — stop-slop is the authority

For AI-tell structure and phrasing — em dashes as sentence punctuation, "not just X, but Y" contrasts, summary-tails, throat-clearing and banned openers, passive voice, metronomic rhythm — apply the `stop-slop` skill. ## Banned vocabulary (the AI-slop dictionary)

delve, tapestry, landscape (figurative), testament, vibrant, pivotal, crucial, intricate, meticulous, bolster, garner, underscore, interplay, multifaceted, nuanced, foster, **leverage** (→ use), **utilize** (→ use), commence (→ start), facilitate, encompass, paramount, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, game-changing, transformative, revolutionize, **seamless / seamlessly**, robust, comprehensive, endeavor, harness, spearhead, unprecedented, remarkable, stunning, profound, synergy.

Banned marketing phrases: "In today's [x] world," "unlock the power of," "elevate your," "streamline your," "supercharge your," "take it to the next level," "bridge the gap," "move the needle," "it's worth noting that," "let's dive in," "at its core," "in the realm of," "when it comes to."

## Filler swaps

"serves as / acts as / functions as" → "is" · "in order to" → "to" · "due to the fact that" → "because" · "at this point in time" → "now" · "in the event that" → "if" · "methodology" → "method" · "functionality" → "feature."

## Bullets vs prose

Bullets only for genuinely parallel lists. Keep argument, cause-and-effect, and narrative as prose — don't fragment reasoning into bullet soup.
